About Dream Initiative

Small acts of care can change a child’s whole future.

We bring people together around practical, compassionate support for orphaned and vulnerable children and the families who care for them.

Stand with a child

Why we exist

Our mission

To empower orphaned and vulnerable children through education, health care, life skills, and the essential support they need to grow with confidence and dignity.

The future we believe in

Our vision

A just and compassionate society where every child has the opportunity to live a healthy, empowered, and dignified life.

How we show up

Care that feels personal

From sharing iftar during the holy month of Ramadan to helping children learn, our work starts with listening and grows through consistent community action.
